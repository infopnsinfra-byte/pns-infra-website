01 / ABOUT P&S INFRA

15+ Years of Manpower Experience. Now a New Chapter with P&S Infra.

Our journey in manpower supply started more than 15 years ago. Since then, our team has been providing skilled and unskilled manpower for construction projects across Delhi NCR and Chandigarh.

Over the years, we have gained practical experience by working closely with construction sites and understanding their real workforce needs. Our experience includes working with reputed names such as:

OUR EXPERIENCE INCLUDES
Simplex | Omaxe | ATS | Godrej | VCL Group

We understand that a successful project needs more than just manpower. It needs reliable people, proper coordination, discipline and a strong focus on safety.

For us, safety is never an option — it is part of the job. We have always focused on safe working practices, proper site discipline and responsible workforce deployment.

Today, we are bringing this 15+ years of manpower experience forward through P&S Infra. We are starting our manpower supply operations for the Adani Power Plant project at Pirpainti, Bihar, in association with the Tata Group.

Our approach is simple: Understand the requirement. Mobilise the right people. Work safely. Support the project.
